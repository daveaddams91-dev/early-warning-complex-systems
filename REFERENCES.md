## 1. Foundational Bifurcation Theory & Critical Slowing Down

1. **Scheffer, M., Bascompte, J., Brock, W. A., Brovkin, V., Carpenter, S. R., Dakos, V., Held, H., van Nes, E. H., Rietkerk, M., & Sugihara, G. (2009).**  
   *Early-warning signals for critical transitions.*  
   **Nature**, 461(7260), 53–59.  
   **DOI**: [10.1038/nature08227](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08227)  
   *Linkage*: Establishes critical slowing down ($\lambda_{\max} \to 0^-$) as the foundational physical mechanism driving variance and autocorrelation divergence in stochastic systems.

2. **Dakos, V., Scheffer, M., van Nes, E. H., Brovkin, V., Petoukhov, V., & Held, H. (2008).**  
   *Slowing down as an early warning signal for abrupt climate change.*  
   **Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS)**, 105(38), 14308–14312.  
   **DOI**: [10.1073/pnas.0802430105](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0802430105)  
   *Linkage*: Formulates rolling AR(1) autocorrelation and variance detrending methods on non-stationary climate paleorecords.

3. **Carpenter, S. R., & Brock, W. A. (2006).**  
   *Rising variance: a leading indicator of ecological transition.*  
   **Ecology Letters**, 9(3), 311–318.  
   **DOI**: [10.1111/j.1461-0248.2005.00877.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1461-0248.2005.00877.x)  
   *Linkage*: Proves analytical variance divergence in whole-lake trophic cascade experiments.

4. **May, R. M. (1977).**  
   *Thresholds and breakpoints in ecosystems with a multiplicity of stable states.*  
   **Nature**, 269(5628), 471–477.  
   **DOI**: [10.1038/269471a0](https://doi.org/10.1038/269471a0)  
   *Linkage*: Mathematical formulation of the 1D grazing/harvesting model with cubic equilibrium geometry (SYS-1).

5. **Stommel, H. (1961).**  
   *Thermohaline convection with two stable regimes of flow.*  
   **Tellus**, 13(2), 224–230.  
   **DOI**: [10.1111/j.2153-3490.1961.tb00079.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2153-3490.1961.tb00079.x)  
   *Linkage*: Two-box ocean circulation model exhibiting saddle-node bifurcation under freshwater forcing (SYS-4).

## 2. Statistical Metrics, Information Theory & Changepoints

6. **Bandt, C., & Pompe, B. (2002).**  
   *Permutation entropy: A natural complexity measure for time series.*  
   **Physical Review Letters**, 88(17), 174102.  
   **DOI**: [10.1103/PhysRevLett.88.174102](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.88.174102)  
   *Linkage*: Ordinal symbolic partition entropy for measuring dynamical complexity loss.

7. **Adams, R. P., & MacKay, D. J. (2007).**  
   *Bayesian online changepoint detection.*  
   **arXiv preprint**, arXiv:0710.3742.  
   **Link**: [https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.3742](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.3742)  
   *Linkage*: Mathematical recursive Bayesian update for online changepoint run lengths (CEWF-BOCPD).

8. **DeLong, E. R., DeLong, D. M., & Clarke-Pearson, D. L. (1988).**  
   *Comparing the areas under two or more correlated receiver operating characteristic curves: a nonparametric approach.*  
   **Biometrics**, 837–845.  
   **DOI**: [10.2307/2531595](https://doi.org/10.2307/2531595)  
   *Linkage*: Mathematical standard for comparing paired ROC-AUC estimators via Mann-Whitney structural components.

## 3. Multivariate, Network & Composite Indicators

9. **Weinans, E., Lever, J. J., Bathiany, S., Quax, R., Bascompte, J., van Nes, E. H., Scheffer, M., & Sloan, C. (2021).**  
   *Finding the direction of resilience loss in multi-dimensional systems.*  
   **Scientific Reports**, 11(1), 14977.  
   **DOI**: [10.1038/s41598-021-94269-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-94269-w)  
   *Linkage*: Demonstrates leading principal component projection for high-dimensional critical slowing down.

10. **Chen, L., Liu, R., Liu, Z. P., Li, M., & Aihara, K. (2012).**  
    *Detecting early-warning signals for sudden deterioration of complex diseases by dynamical network biomarkers.*  
    **Scientific Reports**, 2(1), 342.  
    **DOI**: [10.1038/srep00342](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep00342)  
    *Linkage*: Formulates the Dynamical Network Biomarker (DNB) index based on standard deviations and cross-correlations in leading sub-networks.

11. **Dakos, V., & Bascompte, J. (2014).**  
    *Critical slowing down as early warning for the onset of collapse in mutualistic networks.*  
    **Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS)**, 111(49), 17546–17551.  
    **DOI**: [10.1073/pnas.1406326111](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1406326111)  
    *Linkage*: Formulation of the $N$-node coupled mutualistic network model with logistic self-regulation and saturating functional response (SYS-5).

12. **Bury, T. M., Sujith, R. I., Pavithran, I., Früh, M., Zou, H., Anand, R. S., & Lenton, T. M. (2021).**  
    *Deep learning for early warning signals of critical transitions.*  
    **Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS)**, 118(39), e2106140118.  
    **DOI**: [10.1073/pnas.2106140118](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2106140118)  
    *Linkage*: Machine learning classifiers for bifurcation identification; basis for our novelty contrast.

## 4. Tipping Mechanisms & Falsification Boundaries

13. **Ashwin, P., Wieczorek, S., Vitolo, R., & Cox, P. (2012).**  
    *Tipping points in open systems: bifurcation, noise-induced and rate-dependent examples in the climate system.*  
    **Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society A**, 370(1962), 1166–1184.  
    **DOI**: [10.1098/rsta.2011.0306](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsta.2011.0306)  
    *Linkage*: Mathematical taxonomy formalizing B-tipping (bifurcation), N-tipping (noise-induced), and R-tipping (rate-dependent).

14. **Boettiger, C., & Hastings, A. (2012).**  
    *Early warning signals and the prosecutor's fallacy.*  
    **Proceedings of the Royal Society B: Biological Sciences**, 279(1748), 4734–4739.  
    **DOI**: [10.1098/rspb.2012.2085](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2012.2085)  
    *Linkage*: Establishes the prosecutor's fallacy in EWS and the mandate to test null non-tipping trajectories.

15. **Boettiger, C., Ross, N., & Hastings, A. (2013).**  
    *Early warning signals: the charted and uncharted territories.*  
    **Theoretical Ecology**, 6(3), 255–264.  
    **DOI**: [10.1007/s12080-013-0186-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12080-013-0186-x)  
    *Linkage*: Demonstrates false alarms under non-stationary parameters and changing noise variance.

16. **Ritchie, P., & Sieber, J. (2016).**  
    *Early-warning indicators for rate-induced tipping.*  
    **Chaos: An Interdisciplinary Journal of Nonlinear Science**, 26(9), 093116.  
    **DOI**: [10.1063/1.4962700](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4962700)  
    *Linkage*: Proves that CSD fails under rate-induced tipping because eigenvalues remain non-zero during tracking loss.

17. **Kéfi, S., Guttal, V., Brock, W. A., Carpenter, S. R., Ellison, A. M., Livina, V. N., ... & Dakos, V. (2014).**  
    *Early warning signals of ecological transitions: methods for spatial and temporal data.*  
    **PLoS ONE**, 9(3), e92097.  
    **DOI**: [10.1371/journal.pone.0092097](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0092097)  
    *Linkage*: Formulates intervention timescales and spatial-temporal correlation trade-offs in degraded landscapes.

18. **Bauch, C. T., Sigdel, R., Pharaon, J., & Anand, M. (2016).**  
    *Early warning signals of regime shifts in coupled human-environment systems.*  
    **Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS)**, 113(51), 14560–14567.  
    **DOI**: [10.1073/pnas.1604978113](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1604978113)  
    *Linkage*: Coupled social-ecological dynamics and the optimal timing of management interventions.
